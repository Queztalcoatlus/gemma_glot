Text Cuando era niño, siempre soñaba con viajar por América Latina.

IPA /ˈkwando ˈeɾa ˈniɲo, ˈsjempɾe soˈɲaβa kon βjaˈxaɾ poɾ aˈmeɾika laˈtina/

Translation

When I was a child, I always dreamed of traveling through Latin America.

Syntax

  • Imperfect tense “Era” and “soñaba” describe background states and repeated past experience.
  • Infinitive after preposition “Con viajar” uses an infinitive after “con” to express the content of the dream.

Vocabulary

soñaba A2

Imperfect form of “soñar,” used for recurring or background dreams.

viajar A1

To travel; a high-frequency infinitive for plans and experiences.
